### 🚀 Featured Projects

#### 🧾 Unbilled — AI revenue recovery for construction
SaaS that finds field work contractors did but never billed. Superintendents capture voice notes, photos and measurements; a **six-agent AI chain** (Scope → Quantity → Change → Evidence → Pricing → Billing) turns them into priced, documented change orders with in-platform signing and owner/engineer approval flows.
`Express` `TypeScript` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL 16` `BullMQ` `Redis` `Better Auth` `Cloudflare R2` `React Native` `Claude API` `GitHub Actions`

#### ⚖️ Cairn — IRS Escalation Platform
Multi-tenant AI SaaS for CPA firms managing IRS escalation cases. AI document extraction, case lifecycle engine, DocuSign OAuth per firm (Form 2848), Stripe billing, full audit log, immutable filing history and 7-year retention with Glacier archiving. Human-in-the-loop by design — AI drafts, CPAs decide.
`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL` `Stripe` `DocuSign` `S3` `Claude API`

#### 🤖 SkillForge — AI Agent Marketplace
Marketplace of 40+ AI agents for contractors and local businesses. Each agent runs in an **isolated Docker container** on the Anthropic Agent SDK with MCP tools, persistent sessions, BullMQ-scheduled tasks and a per-second Stripe credit wallet.
`Next.js` `Agent SDK` `MCP` `Docker` `BullMQ` `Redis` `Stripe` `Hetzner`

#### 📣 SocialMint.ai — Social Scheduling
Live social scheduling platform with OAuth for Meta, X and Google. Also acts as the OAuth bridge powering SkillForge's social agents.
`Next.js` `OAuth 2.0` `X API v2` `Meta Graph API` `PostgreSQL`

#### 🚗 Auto Parts Compatibility Platform
Multi-tenant NZ automotive parts platform — master catalogue of makes/models/years/parts, compatibility mapping (incl. JDM chassis codes), subscriber search API, per-tenant inventory and Stripe subscriptions. Plus a **live e-commerce store** wired to Stripe, Xero, Pinnacle and CarJam.
`Next.js` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `Stripe` `Xero` `Pinnacle API`

#### 🎬 Video Platform
Video processing pipeline built on **FFmpeg** — transcoding, encoding and media delivery.
`FFmpeg` `Node.js`

#### 🎨 AI Generator
Image/media generation tooling over FLUX, Gemini and local diffusion models, plus local vision pipelines (OCR + VLM) on GPU.
`Python` `PyTorch` `CUDA` `FLUX` `ComfyUI`

#### 🧠 RAG Systems
Retrieval pipelines for document Q&A and AI agents — embeddings, tool-based search and long-document strategies.
`Embeddings` `Postgres` `Ollama` `LLMs`
